01 — Who we are

We build machines that think, move and learn.

ROBOZIK is a robotics lab at the intersection of mechatronics and embodied AI. Since 2019 we've shipped 128 machines into factories, laboratories and places humans shouldn't go.

Our teams sit in one room — mechanical, electrical, firmware and machine learning — so a decision that used to take a month takes an afternoon.

04 — How we work

From brief to boot-up.

A tight loop of design, simulation and field testing. Most programs hit a working prototype in 10–14 weeks.

01

Brief

A working session, not a sales call. We map constraints, budget and what success actually looks like.

02

Simulate

Digital twin first. Thousands of cycles in physics before a single chip is cut.

03

Prototype

Instrumented hardware in weeks, not quarters — then pushed until it breaks, and rebuilt.

04

Deploy

Certification, operator training and live telemetry. Then we stay on call.
